Age rating and content

Sandstone Kingdom: Tower Defense is designed for players aged 7 and up. The official rating for your country comes from the IARC questionnaire in Google Play and is shown on the store page.

TopicIn the game
ViolenceCartoon fantasy combat. Enemies vanish in a puff of dust. No blood or gore.
LanguageNone
FearMild: boss enemies such as the Bandit King
In-app purchasesYes: optional ruby packs, a starter pack and Remove Ads. The whole story can be finished free.
AdsYes: optional reward ads, rare ads between levels, a banner on the map. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens (rated T or lower).
Loot boxesNone. Every chest shows exactly what is inside.
Online interactionNo chat. Optional leaderboards show Google Play Games display names.
DataSee the privacy policy.

For parents

Purchases go through Google Play, where you can require a password or parental approval with Google Family Link.
