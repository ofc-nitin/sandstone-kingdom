Privacy Policy

Sandstone Kingdom: Tower Defense · Samvardhan Studios · Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what data the game Sandstone Kingdom: Tower Defense (the "Game", Android package com.samvardhanstudios.sandstonekingdom) uses, why, and the choices you have. Samvardhan Studios ("we") is responsible for the Game.

1. Data stored on your phone

Your progress (levels, diyas, coins, rubies, settings and statistics) is saved only on your phone. You do not need an account to play. You can delete it at any time in Settings > Delete my data, or by uninstalling the Game.

2. Google Play Games (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, Google shares your Play Games player ID and display name with the Game. We use them to show your achievements and leaderboard ranks and to save your progress in your Google account (Saved Games), so it moves with you to a new phone. Google's handling of this data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Public endless leaderboard (optional)

If you are signed in, your Play Games display name, player ID, best endless score and wave may be stored in a public top-100 list so that every player can see the rankings. Contact us to have your entry removed.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads from Google AdMob: optional reward ads, occasional full-screen ads between levels, and a small banner on the map screen. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. It uses them to show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, Google's consent form asks for your choices before personalised ads are shown. You can change them any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses data in ads.

5. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. We receive a purchase confirmation (product and order token) to deliver what you bought and to restore it later.

6. Crash and diagnostics data

Google Play may give us anonymous crash reports and performance statistics about the Game, which we use only to fix problems.

7. What we do not do

We do not sell your personal data. We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, camera or microphone. The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

8. Retention and your rights

On-device data stays until you delete it. Cloud saves stay in your Google account until you delete them in your Google Play Games settings. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its use. Email us and we will reply within 30 days.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. Questions or requests: support@samvardhanstudios.com.
