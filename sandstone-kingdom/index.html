Sandstone Kingdom: Tower Defense

The Bandit King has stolen the Sun Crown, and without it spring will never return. Build archer towers, barracks of Holy Knights, mage towers and cannons along the roads of medieval India. Lead the Sun Knight into battle and call down Arrow Rain and Holy Fire.

Three ways to play

Story

A journey through six regions and six seasons of India: the Aravalli Hills, Thar Desert, Western Ghats, Deccan Ruins, Sundarbans and the Himalayas. Every fifth level is a boss fight.

Challenges

Every level you finish unlocks three challenges, such as Bowmen Only or Iron Fort with a single life. Challenges pay rubies.

Endless

Beat a region's boss to unlock its Endless mode. How many waves can you hold? Climb the leaderboards.

Screenshots

Fair to play

The whole story can be finished without paying. There are no loot boxes and no energy timers. Ads between levels are rare and never shown after a loss, and you can remove them for good.
