# Making Sandstone Kingdom feel alive

The Founder asked: what else would make the game feel real and alive, "not like pictures shooting arrows"?
This list covers what is now in the game (**done**) and what comes next, in order of value.

## Sound and music

| Idea | Status |
|---|---|
| Music that follows the battle: calm before the first wave, faster with more drums while fighting, a deep war-horn drone and pounding dhol when the fort is in danger or on the final wave, a buzzing shehnai lead for bosses | **done** |
| A heartbeat when only a few lives are left | **done** |
| A war horn and battle cries when each wave starts; a drum roll and fanfare for the FINAL WAVE | **done** |
| Birdsong and wind in the Aravalli hills | **done** |
| Distinct sounds for shields going up and breaking, smoke bombs, portals, the elephant's trumpet, bubbling poison, upgrades and special spots opening | **done** |
| Region ambience for later regions: desert wind and camel bells, monsoon rain, river frogs, Himalayan wind | next, with each region |
| Short voice barks for the Sun Knight ("For the Sun!") and enemy shouts, as synthesised grunts | next |
| A different victory theme for bosses, and a quiet "aftermath" melody on defeat | next |

## Movement and feedback

| Idea | Status |
|---|---|
| Walk cycle for every soldier, hero and enemy; riders bob and tilt | **done** |
| Attack swings when fighting; archers loose arrows on the towers; cannons recoil with a muzzle flash; mage towers flare when they cast | **done** |
| Enemies fall over and fade when defeated, flash white when hit | **done** |
| Towers rise out of the ground when built; on upgrade they pop, a pillar of golden light rises and "UPGRADED!" appears | **done** |
| Arrows leave a faint trail; cannon blasts leave scorch marks that fade | **done** |
| Shields shown as a glowing bubble with their own bar; war-drum beat rings; poison bubbles; jammed towers smoking | **done** |
| Slow motion when the boss walks in and when the last enemy falls; a red pulse at the screen edge when the fort is in danger | **done** |
| Next-wave preview: enemy icons above every entrance marker | **done** |
| Floating damage numbers, with bigger gold numbers for critical hits (with an option to turn them off) | next |
| Soldiers celebrate (raise weapons) after a wave is cleared | next |
| A short camera pan over the map at the start of each level, showing the entrances | next |

## The world

| Idea | Status |
|---|---|
| Cloud shadows drifting over the battlefield, birds crossing, butterflies, spring petals | **done** |
| A live main menu: turning sun rays, drifting clouds, birds, flickering gate torches, breathing heroes, a sword glint, petals | **done** |
| Portals that swirl where enemies vanish and reappear | **done** |
| Animated water (stepwell, pond), swaying trees and flags, chimney smoke from the village | next |
| Weather per region: sandstorms (towers see less), monsoon rain (slower enemies), night with torches (Deccan), fog (Sundarbans), snow (Himalayas) | next, with each region |
| Villagers and animals reacting: villagers run into houses when a wave starts, cows wander, the peacock dances when you win | next |
| A day-to-dusk light shift across a level's waves | later |

## Story and pacing

| Idea | Status |
|---|---|
| Battle briefing with a short, casual story line before every level | **done** |
| A short comic-style cutscene before each boss (3–4 panels drawn from the game art) | next |
| The Royal Sage pops in with a one-line hint when a new enemy appears or the player struggles (for example after losing twice) | next |
| A boss health bar across the top with the boss's name; boss phases (the Bandit King calls more bandits under half health) | next |
| End-of-region celebration: fireworks over the fort and the region's season changing on the map | next |
