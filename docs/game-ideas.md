# Mobile Game Ideas: trending, monetizable, easy to build with AI (Unity)

_Researched 2026-11-02. Market figures come from secondary write-ups of Sensor Tower / AppMagic
reports and may not match the originals exactly; treat them as directional._

## What's trending

- **Mobile, hybrid-casual is the main growth story.** These are simple games that earn from
  ads *and* in-app purchases. In H1 2026 hybrid-casual in-app revenue grew roughly 20–37%
  (figures vary by source), while mid-core declined.
- **Sort, screw and block puzzles are the hottest sub-genre**, with reported revenue
  growth of about 46%. **Merge-2** revenue nearly doubled, to about $1.24B.
- **Ads alone no longer pay well.** Successful casual games mix rewarded ads with
  purchases such as "remove ads", hints and boosters.
- **On PC, roguelikes keep breaking out**, and their mechanics port well to mobile. Survivors-likes (Megabonk, a solo
  developer's game that passed 1M sales in two weeks), roguelike deckbuilders (Slay the
  Spire 2) and tower defense (Tangy TD, also a solo developer) all did well.
- **Hyper-casual (one-tap, ads-only) is declining.** Avoid it as a pure play.

## Ranked shortlist (mobile: Android + iOS)

Build ease: ★★★ = easiest with AI help. Every game below suits Unity.

| # | Game type | How it makes money | Trend | Build ease | Time to first playable* |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sort puzzle** (ball / water / screw-nut sort) | Rewarded ads + "remove ads" + hint packs | 🔥 Fastest-growing | ★★★ | 1–2 weeks |
| 2 | **Block puzzle** (Block Blast–style 8×8 grid) | Ads + IAP continues | 🔥 High | ★★★ | 1–2 weeks |
| 3 | **Idle / incremental tycoon** | Rewarded ads for boosts, IAP | Steady | ★★★ | 1–2 weeks |
| 4 | **Daily word / logic puzzle** | Ads, optional subscription | Steady (daily habit) | ★★★ | 1 week |
| 5 | **Survivors-like** (auto-attack hordes, pick upgrades; portrait, one thumb) | Rewarded ads (revive, double loot) + IAP | 🔥 High | ★★☆ | 3–5 weeks |
| 6 | **Merge-2** (merge items to complete orders) | IAP energy/boosters, ads | 🔥 Revenue doubled | ★★☆ (needs economy tuning + art) | 4–6 weeks |
| 7 | **Tower defense** | IAP + rewarded ads | Good | ★★☆ | 4–6 weeks |

\*Rough estimates for one person working with AI. Polish, art and store launch add time.

**Skip for a first game:** multiplayer games (servers and netcode are hard), 3D
open worlds, and pure hyper-casual one-tap games (ads-only, declining).

## Recommendation

1. **Start with #1, a sort puzzle, in Unity for Android first, then iOS.** It has the
   strongest current trend data, simple logic that AI writes well, levels that can be
   generated by code, and a monetization model (Unity LevelPlay ads + Unity IAP) that's
   already proven. It's the lowest-risk way to learn the full build → publish → earn loop.
2. **Then build #5, a mobile survivors-like.** It has higher engagement and IAP
   potential, and reuses the Unity and monetization setup from game 1.

## Honest caveats

- No game type guarantees income. Most revenue comes from **marketing and retention**
  (whether players come back on day 1 and day 7), not just from shipping.
- Store publishing costs money: Google Play charges a $25 one-time fee and Apple
  charges $99 per year. You also need a Mac (or a cloud Mac build service) to build for iOS.
- Earning from ads takes volume. Expect small amounts until you have thousands of
  daily players.
