Garh Rakshakगढ़ रक्षक · Fort Defense

Concept board 02. A tower defense game painted in the style of Rajasthan's Phad scrolls: medieval hero-epics painted on long cotton cloths in flat vermilion, saffron, green and indigo with strong black outlines. You defend a hill fort against the Shadow Raja's raiders.

samvardhana studios · nov 2026
concept only · not final art

Sample screen. Defenders wear white jamas and saffron or red turbans; the Shadow Raja's band wears indigo-black with face cloths and always faces the fort. Gold mohurs are money, lit diyas are lives, and the sun walking to the moon counts the waves.

The villains are a fictional band of outlaws, not any real kingdom, community or religion. That keeps the game safe to sell worldwide and respectful at home.

Level select: the Phad scroll

A real Phad is unrolled at night and sung episode by episode by a storyteller holding a lamp. Our level select works the same way: each won level paints its episode onto the scroll, and the lamp shows which episode is next.
