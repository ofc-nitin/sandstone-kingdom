Live scene: the raiders really walk the road (open the HTML to see it move). Camera is fixed and tilted like looking down at a toy fort on a table: easy to read and cheap to render on low-end phones.

Why wooden toys

Truly 3D, truly IndianTurned-wood lacquer toys from Channapatna (Karnataka) and Kondapalli (Andhra), and Rajasthan's kathputli puppets, are already "3D Indian art". No other tower defense game looks like this.
Easy for code to buildEvery toy is made of turned shapes (cylinders, spheres, flared skirts) plus painted lacquer rings. Code can generate the whole cast in one consistent style, no 3D artist needed.
Readable and fastFew polygons, simple materials and one light: runs at 60 fps on cheap phones. Toys on round bases read like game pieces, so players instantly see what's a tower.

The cast: defenders (left) and raiders (right)

Defenders wear lime-white jamas with saffron or red turbans and stand on light wood bases. Raiders are dark indigo lacquer with silver rings on dark bases. Upgrades add rings, plumes and gold.
