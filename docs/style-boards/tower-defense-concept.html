Concept board. A tower defense game painted in Warli: the village defends its granary from pests and storms by placing villagers along painted paths.

concept 01 · samvardhana studios
nov 2026 · not final art

Sample screen, portrait, one thumb. Our villagers are rice-paste white; every threat is charcoal ink, so friend and foe read instantly. The sun crossing the sky is the wave counter, rice bowls are money, sacks are lives.

Level select: the village wall

Each defended level paints its scene onto one long wall that scrolls sideways. The next level is only a sketch until you win it. By the end, the player has painted a whole village.
